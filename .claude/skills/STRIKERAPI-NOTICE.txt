Skills in this folder (backtesting, dispatch, edge-detection, elo-ratings, goalscorer-prop-modeling,
match-lookup, match-preview, odds-analysis, odds-explorer, player-scouting, poisson-score-models,
probability-calibration, strikerapi-tool, team-analysis, walk-forward-validation, xg-analysis)
are from https://github.com/noahowsh/strikerapi-mcp (commit a7069b1), MIT licensed, see STRIKERAPI-LICENSE.
They call the hosted StrikerAPI MCP server and need STRIKERAPI_API_KEY there; the methodology skills
(Poisson, calibration, backtesting) also work without it.
